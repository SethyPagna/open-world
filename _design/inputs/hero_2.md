# The Case for the Glow-Frog (working name: **Glim**)

## Verdict

I recommend the glow-frog hybrid as the hero. Of the three options it is the only one that keeps the GDD's first pillar ("Light is life and language") and also puts the user's own image, "frog in a well, comes out of well sees the sky", into the player's body. With the candle, the idiom only appears in the scenery. The hybrid costs a real rewrite of hero-facing systems. It does not cost a new game. The rooms, Draft, Tide, Crown, Ledger, economy and the world structure all stay as they are. Three things still need proving: it is harder to read state from a frog than from a shrinking candle, the tongue can break the balance of grabbing and Knives, and frogs carry a brand-safety risk. The gates for those are listed at the end.

---

## 1. Fidelity: the player *is* the idiom

In the user's newest message the frog is the protagonist of the metaphor: it "comes out of well sees the sky, then go to sky sees space." The current documents moved the frog into a statue at the Foot and the last silhouette on the rim (WORLD_STRUCTURE §0 row 3, §10.5). That is elegant, but it asks the player to identify with a candle while the story is about a frog.

With Glims, every reveal is about *you*:
- **★1 "Your sky was a hole."** The camera pulls back from a crowd of small glowing frogs at the well lip. That is the idiom played out literally.
- **The Revelation.** The enormous lantern-holding frog leaning over the rim becomes *your species, scaled up*, so "Every sky is someone's well" lands on the player's own body.
- **Cycle 2.** "The frog has gone up" stops being a lore line about a statue and becomes a statement about the player.

The Foot statue gets stronger too. It becomes the first Glim, worn smooth "where someone kept pointing up."

## 2. It keeps pillar 1 (this is what separates it from the pure frog)

The glow sits in the throat sac and in a row of spots along the spine. It is the frog's light, face and health, so the dark-room design, the light-seeking monsters, the brightness rating and the Snuffer all survive. Most mappings only change a noun:

| GDD (Wick) | Glim | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Wax 0–100, body shrinks (`0.55 + 0.45×wax/max`) | **Glow** 0–100. The same formula deflates the puffed body, and 10 spine spots go dark, one per 10 Glow | Noun, plus a spot overlay |
| Flame PointLight scales with wax | Sac light scales with Glow (6 / 14 / 2 m) | None |
| Brighten (F) / Dim (Q) | Inflate sac / close sac to eye-shine | Animation only |
| Drip (R, 5 per tap) | **Boop**: nose-to-nose glow transfer | Animation only |
| Snuffed → Holder puddle → Smoke | Goes dark, leaving a **frogspawn egg**, and becomes a **tadpole wisp** | Re-skin |
| Relight (2 s, match flick) | **Hatch**: croak into the egg | Re-skin |
| Soot bands at 30/50/70% | Three soot stripes across the flanks, drawn on top, never over the spine spots | Re-place |
| Snuffer: a bell that drops on the brightest light | A bronze **bell-jar**: the childhood firefly jar, house-sized | Stronger image, same rules |

The System copy mostly survives as written: `[ WELCOME, GLIM ] You have been lit. Please do not go out.`

## 3. Signature verbs (the real upgrade)

The GDD's movement list already reads like a frog: hop-waddle run, belly-flop dive, squat brace. On a candle those are borrowed. On a frog they belong to the body. The hybrid adds two verbs a candle cannot have:

- **Tongue-lash** (it replaces the Grab cone's reach, not the whole grab system).
  - Reach is 3.0 m, against Grab's 1.6 m.
  - It has a 0.25 s tell: the sac bulges and the eyes lock on. The tongue is a glowing Tube that everyone can see.
  - It pulls props of 20 kg or less to you, and pulls you to anchors of 60 kg or more. That gives you gap-swings in C rooms and grapple-drift in Space.
  - It only *tugs* a climber 1 m, unless they are your Pact partner.
  - Stealing a held item fails if the holder is gripping, so gripping works as a Cinch.
  - Result: every tongue-steal is a Knife with a readable tell, which keeps pillar 4 intact.
- **Croak.**
  - **Tap** sends a pulse of light and sound: 8 m for 0.4 s, costs 2 Glow, and gives +2 brightness rating for 1 s. That flashes wax-script into view briefly but draws light-seekers, so it is a risk.
  - **Chorus.** Three or more Glims croaking within 0.3 s of each other double the radius for free. Real frog choruses do line up their calls, and here it becomes a co-op rhythm verb.
  - **Hold** sustains a pitch. The GDD's sound riddles already call for this: the Belfry is "sing a bronze golem apart", and the Harmony Sentinel needs harmony. Rooms opt in, and none of them requires it.
- **Tongue-tied.** The Pact thread runs mouth to mouth. The pun sells itself and fits the KNOTSPIRE name.

## 4. Readability from silhouette (the honest weak point)

The candle's height-as-HP is the best visible health bar I have seen in a design, and a frog cannot match it one for one. The hybrid makes up for it in layers:

1. **HP** is shown three ways: the body deflates (the same formula), the spine spots count down, and the sac light dims. A top-down or 3/4 camera sees a frog's broad back better than a candle's narrow top. That helps in the 70° Draft Cam, where the question is who is on which plate.
2. **Emotion** has fewer shape options, because a sac cannot fork the way a flame does. It moves to three channels:
   - sac rhythm: Calm 0.5 Hz breathing, Fear 6 Hz flutter, Joy bounce with sparks;
   - eyelids and pupils;
   - posture: Sad slumps sideways with a 20% blue tint.
3. **Resolve**, the critical tell, stays unmistakable. The tongue tip peeks out and glows crimson, the body leans toward the target, and the sac shifts 15% toward crimson.
4. **Notorious** still burns violet through walls.

Fairness holds. Every Glim uses the same 0.32 m capsule, the same sac height and the same body, whatever the skin.

## 5. Surviving the scale-up

- **L1 Well.** The frog is at home. The Tide is still a threat because the well water is black and cold: submerged Glims lose 2 Glow/s.
- **L2 Sky (UPDRAFT).** Webbed feet spread into glide membranes. Wallace's flying frog does this in nature. The hop becomes a glide-hop, and the Omen doubles glide range.
- **L3 Space (DRIFT).** A breaststroke works in zero-g, and the tongue becomes a grapple off bulkheads. A glow needs no oxygen. A candle flame in a vacuum needs a hand-wave in the fiction.
- **L4 Beyond (LIGHT-LAG).** Croak pulses travel slowly and echo back late, so the Omen can be heard as well as seen.

Honest note: no real frog glows. Some fluoresce, like the pumpkin toadlet. This is fantasy that is anchored in real biology, and that is enough.

## 6. Buildability and animation (Three.js, all primitives)

- **Body.** A scaled sphere with a vertex belly-bulge and the same SSS wrap-lighting planned for wax. Glass-frog skins get this treatment for free.
- **Eyes.** Two spheres plus half-sphere lids. The blink every 3–6 s is already specified.
- **Sac.** A sphere on a damped spring. A croak inflates it to 1.8× in 0.12 s and the spring gives free jiggle as secondary motion.
- **Legs.** Four 2-bone capsule chains with analytic IK. That is the same reach solver the GDD already uses for the nub hands.
- **Tongue.** A TubeGeometry along a 10-point verlet chain with a sticky sphere at the tip.
- **Hop cycle.** This is where squash and stretch comes from: anticipation squash 15%, launch stretch 20% with the legs trailing, then a 20% landing splat with a sac wobble.

Cost estimate (my estimate, not a measured one): about 1.5–2 extra tech-art weeks over the Lathe-and-nubs Wick, mostly for leg IK and tongue tuning.

## 7. Skins and commercial appeal

- **Silhouette.** A squat dome, two eye bumps and a glowing throat. It reads at thumbnail size the way the Among Us bean and the Fall Guys jellybean do.
- **Rarity ladder from nature.** Bright colours mean danger in real frogs, so dart-frog palettes (strawberry, blue-jeans, bumblebee) are Tallow/Beeswax. Glass frog, where you see the glow through the skin, is Tapered. Gilded frog is Gilded.
- **The existing catalogue mostly survives** (my estimate):
  - **Unchanged, 21 items:** Shadow, Oath Thread, Betrayal Snap and System Window.
  - **Moved onto the frog, about 30 items:**
    - Wax becomes **Skin**. Honeycomb, Speckled Egg, Stained Glass, Night Sky and Porcelain Crackle all suit a frog.
    - Flame becomes **Glow**, which covers the sac and the eye-shine.
    - Wick becomes **Crest**. The Traffic-Cone and Coiled Spring items work as hats.
    - Holder becomes **Seat**: the Teacup, a lily pad, the Chamberstick.
  - **Re-authored, about 9 items:** Smoke becomes tadpole wisps, and Knock becomes **Croak voice**.
- **Croak voices** are a new kind of item to sell. A frog in a teacup is merch that sells itself.

## Fair critique of the alternatives

**Candle Wick (current GDD).**
- **Strengths.** It is the most complete option: fully specified, 60 items built, and no rewrite. It has the best HP readability of any option. The flame as a face is expressive and original. The name fits: a "knot" is a wick thread.
- **Weaknesses.**
  - It moves the user's central image off the player's body.
  - Its unique verbs (Brighten, Dim, Drip) are ones the hybrid keeps anyway. Beyond those, it is a Fall Guys bean with a flame head.
  - The fiction strains in water (the Tide) and in space.
  - It has lower mascot appeal than an animal with eyes and a mouth.
  - Candle heroes already exist, for example *Candleman*.

**Pure frog.**
- **Strengths.** It is the most faithful to the idiom, has the strongest mascot and the best verbs.
- **Weakness.** It breaks pillar 1. Without a body light, the dark rooms need a carried lantern. HP becomes a HUD bar, and the light-seekers, Brighten/Dim and the Snuffer all need new designs. This repeats Draft v0.1's mistake: Review 2's F1 found that the draft replaced the game.

**A possible 4th option: the wax-frog candle,** a frog-shaped candle with its wick on its head.
- **Strengths.** It needs no system changes and gives a frog silhouette.
- **Why I do not think it is better.** It is a candle in a frog costume. Wax cannot croak or lash a tongue, so the idiom is only on the surface.
- **Where it belongs.** It is the right fallback if the rewrite is refused. Ship it as a "Toad Votive" Origin, since Origins already change only the Lathe profile.

## Costs and gates (what must be true before we commit)

1. **Rewrite size.** These parts change:
   - GDD §0, §1.1 (pillar 1 wording), §2.1, §4.3–4.5, §5.1–5.3, §12.2, §13.4–13.6, §15 SFX;
   - the in-game noun;
   - WORLD_STRUCTURE §0 row 3.

   These do not change: rooms, numbers, the Draft, the economy and the world layout.
2. **Readability gate.** In the dark at 25 m, testers must identify HP to within 20% and the emotion. The Glim must score within 10% of the Wick's accuracy.
3. **Tongue gate.** Across 500 bot runs, the Knife rate per floor must stay within ±15% of the Grab baseline.
4. **Brand gate.** Five cold viewers make no Pepe association. Art rules: round shapes, dart-frog colours, no lips, no droopy lids.

If gate 2 fails, keep the candle and ship the Toad Votive Origin. If gates 2–4 pass, the Glim is the stronger hero.
