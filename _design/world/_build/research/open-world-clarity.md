# Open-world clarity: how small teams make a world feel open, clear and easy to read

## Draft notes gathered so far (to be expanded)

Session 1 notes (not yet re-checked unless marked CHECKED):

- BotW CEDEC 2017 "triangle rule": CHECKED (gist + GIGAZINE). Large = landmark, medium = hides landmark and route, small = tempo. Rectangles hide fully. Gravity: attraction objects of varied visibility and importance pull in different directions. Heat maps: first bunched paths, many unvisited areas; after placing attraction objects between towers, routes spread; but players then said it felt "guided"; fix = vary visibility and importance. Map: 120 dynamically loadable areas, 4 zoom levels, 2,344 map screens, generated not hand made. Only 2 UI designers; LayoutEditor tool. Sources: https://gist.github.com/idbrii/e39fe96279aa1670319bfa521d907399 , https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20171004-breath-of-the-wild-design-secret/
- Great Plateau: tutorial plateau, cannot leave without paraglider, 4 shrines. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Plateau (not yet re-checked)
- TotK Ask the Developer vol 9 part 3: CHECKED. Sky islands looked like "loose specks of trash" from the ground when many and small; had to be made visually prominent. Surface, sky and underground seamless, no loading screens; sound team needed a line for where "the sky begins". https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/ask-the-developer-vol-9-the-legend-of-zelda-tears-of-the-kingdom-part-3/
- TotK depths (Nintendo Everything citing the Japanese art book): CHECKED. Depths not planned at first; deep caves were not surprising enough; idea of an underground the same size as the overworld; rule = invert the surface map (rivers become high walls, high points become low points) so it was makeable at realistic cost; prototype video convinced Aonuma. https://nintendoeverything.com/zelda-tears-of-the-kingdom-depths-origins-interview/
- Ghost of Tsushima wind: CHECKED (PS Blog, Jason Connell). Wind came from an art goal of movement in everything; years in, they tried it as navigation to objectives and collectibles. https://blog.playstation.com/2020/05/29/ghost-of-tsushima-your-questions-answered/ ; GDC 2021 wind talk by Bill Rockenbeck https://gdcvault.com/play/1027124/Blowing-from-the-West-Simulating (description only)
- Ghost of Tsushima streaming: GDC 2021 "Zen of Streaming" (Adrian Bentley): world about 15x larger than earlier Sucker Punch games; fine-grained streaming model (search summary, Supported). https://gdcvault.com/play/1027545/Zen-of-Streaming-Building-and ; PDF too large to fetch.
- A Short Hike: CHECKED (Wikipedia). Solo dev Adam Robinson-Yu, Unity, Yarn Spinner, Cinemachine; started Dec 2018; 3-month commit after Humble funding; alpha/beta/RC phases; min 7 golden feathers for the set summit route, fewer via harder paths; IGF 2020 Seumas McNally Grand Prize. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Short_Hike . GDC talk: curiosity landmarks, natural barriers, flexible scope with stretch goals, simplified scrum, pixelated render, palettes from Canadian Shield fall photos (Podwise summary, Supported) https://podwise.ai/episodes/1459231 , https://gdcvault.com/play/1026613/Independent-Games-Summit-Crafting-A . No map; signs "follow signs to Hawk Peak"; every area memorable via landmark, quest, camera framing, level variation (Medium analysis, Supported) https://medium.com/@rwmthethird/architectural-analysis-a-short-hike-05037af87142 . Cave loop back to town: [unverified].
- Outer Wilds (Kelsey Beachum GDC 2021 PDF): CHECKED from the primary deck. 22-minute loop; knowledge only reward; no gating; big visible events (cyclones, probe launch, exploding sun) show not tell; push vs pull info (text forced only at very start and end); unvisited text looks different; clue text grouped by location; travellers give pull hints ("start with something small" -> the moon); 30 min to 2-3 h before players investigate Nomai text; ship log only records what player surely knows; small location-bound arcs; three content levels: surface, mid, hidden (answers always hidden). https://media.gdcvault.com/GDC+2021/beachum_gdc_2021(1).pdf
- Sky: other players hidden until revealed by your candle; hand-holding lets veterans guide newcomers; benches hard to reach; cooperative goals make players treat others as tools. https://www.pockettactics.com/sky-children-of-the-light/friends ; https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1026818/Designing-Social-Play-for-Sky (not yet re-checked)
