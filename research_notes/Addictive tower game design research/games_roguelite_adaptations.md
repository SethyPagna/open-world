# "One More Run" Roguelites and Tower/System/Door Adaptations: How They Make Progress Feel Constant and Addictive

Scope note: Research done September 2026. Several primary pages (Fandom wikis, Game Developer, PocketGamer.biz, the official DOORS wiki, NamuWiki) blocked fetching (HTTP 402/403/429), so some facts below come from search-result snippets of those pages rather than full reads. They are marked "(snippet)". Each "Inferences" bullet ends with an **Implication** for our game: a Three.js browser game for 1-8 players with bots, lettered sub-level doors (1A, 1B...), gimmick monsters with odd clear conditions, powers, team-ups and betrayal, hub minigames, a cosmetic shop and concentric world layers (Well -> Sky -> Space).

## Q1. How do the best roguelites structure a run (length, decision and reward frequency, power curve, synergies, "broken build" moments) and meta progression (unlocks, heat/ascension, narrative between runs)?

### Takeaway
The hits share one skeleton. Runs are short (about 15-35 minutes) and made of small numbered steps. Every step ends in a pick-1-of-3 reward, and power grows within the run until synergies let the player "break" the game. Around that sits meta progression that pays out even on a loss: currency, unlocks, harder difficulty tiers and, in Hades, new story. The strongest 2025-2026 sellers (Slay the Spire 2, Hades II, Megabonk, Ball x Pit) all keep this skeleton.

### Cited Findings
**Hades / Hades II (Supergiant)**
- An explicit early design goal was "to take the pain out of dying and having to restart", so that "the moment of death isn't about rage-quitting" and players "feel the time you spent wasn't a waste of your time" (Greg Kasavin). — [Game Developer: How Supergiant weaves narrative rewards into Hades' cycle of perpetual death](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/how-supergiant-weaves-narrative-rewards-into-i-hades-i-cycle-of-perpetual-death) (snippet; full page returned 403)
- Hades ties story progress to finishing or failing runs, not to reaching a set objective, so death becomes part of the storytelling. — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/how-supergiant-weaves-narrative-rewards-into-i-hades-i-cycle-of-perpetual-death) (snippet)
- A studio of fewer than 20 people shipped a fully voiced game with more than 22,000 lines of dialogue. The developers say Early Access was "a huge boon for the narrative". — [GDC Vault: Breathing Life into Greek Myth: The Dialogue of Hades](https://gdcvault.com/play/1027149/Breathing-Life-into-Greek-Myth)
- Olympian gods give "Boons". When the player takes one, they choose 1 of 3 effects, and combining different gods' boons produces synergies that keep runs fresh. Players choose between 1-3 chamber exits after each room. — [TheGamer: Hades' Boon System Is The Perfect Answer To Replayability](https://www.thegamer.com/hades-boon-replay-escape/); [Hades Wiki: Chambers and Encounters](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Chambers_and_Encounters) (snippet)
- Hades sold more than 1,000,000 copies, 700,000 of them during Early Access (which ran from December 2018 to the September 17, 2020 launch). — [Supergiant Games on X](https://x.com/SupergiantGames/status/1307744738552938496); [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/hades-has-sold-1-million-copies-nearly-one-third-sold-in-last-few-days/1100-6482388/-01-10abi2f/)

**Balatro (LocalThunk)**
- Run structure: each "ante" has three rounds (Small Blind, Big Blind, Boss Blind), and beating ante 8 wins the run, with an endless mode afterwards. ### Inferences
- **The universal atom is "clear a small numbered step, then pick 1 of 3".** Hades has chambers and boons, Balatro has blinds and the shop, StS has floors and card rewards, and Vampire Survivors and Megabonk have level-ups. The player makes a meaningful choice every 30-120 seconds, and each choice changes the build. **Implication:** Every cleared lettered sub-level (1A, 1B...) should end with a visible pick-1-of-3: a power, a modifier, a relic or a "System skill". The choice of which door to take next should preview its reward type on the door itself, the way Hades' chamber exits show a reward icon. Door choice is then a build decision, not a blind guess.
- **"Breakable" design is a feature.** Balatro, Vampire Survivors and Hades all let synergies stack multiplicatively until the player feels overpowered for a moment, then raise difficulty to meet it (Boss Blinds, heat and stakes, 30-minute waves). **Implication:** Design powers to combine. A "Duo power" when two teamed players stack abilities (like Hades' Duo boons, which mix two gods) gives teaming up a mechanical payoff, and betraying a partner costs you the combo. Let late-tower floors show absurd numbers.
- **Death must pay.** Hades (story), Vampire Survivors (gold and achievements), Balatro (unlocks) and StS (Ascension unlocks) all give something on a loss. **Implication:** Every failed climb must award soft currency, codex entries (monster rules discovered), "furthest door" records and hub story beats. A wipe should never be the end of a session.
- **Difficulty ladders (Heat, Ascension, Stakes) keep veterans playing.** **Implication:** After a player clears a layer (say Well), unlock "Tower Pressure" levels: opt-in modifiers such as darker rooms, faster monsters, fewer revives or rival bots, each with a badge and a cosmetic reward.
- **Early Access plus live metrics produced both Hades and StS.** **Implication:** Log death-by-monster, door-choice and clear-time telemetry per sub-level from day one, and tune weekly, as StS did.
- **Short runs sell.** Megabonk and Ball x Pit are cheap, fast, and have bright feedback. **Implication:** A full run of one layer should fit in 15-25 minutes, with a quick-restart path under 10 seconds.

### Gaps
- Exact Hades run length and chambers per run could not be verified from a primary source. Snippets suggested roughly 25-35 minutes for a successful run and one gave "69 chambers", which looks unreliable.
- Risk of Rain 2 (difficulty scales with time spent, which pushes speed), Dead Cells and Brotato were not researched in this session for lack of budget, so no cited data exists for them here.
- No primary source was found for Balatro's or Megabonk's median run length or retention curves.

## Q2. Why is Roblox DOORS so successful (player counts, monster-rule design, co-op fear and comedy, door numbering, short sessions, revive/Knobs economy)?

### Takeaway
DOORS is the closest existing proof of our concept. It is a numbered sequence of rooms (100 in the Hotel), and each rare entity has one telegraphed survival rule that players learn by dying. Runs happen in small co-op groups, a Knobs soft currency feeds a timed pre-run shop, and Robux sells revives and boosts. New floors and big updates drive large player-count spikes years after launch: its all-time recorded peak of 226,937 players came on August 28, 2026, four years after release.

### Cited Findings
**Scale and timeline**
- LSPLASH (with Redibles) released DOORS on August 10, 2022 with "The Hotel" floor. The Hotel+ revamp followed January 29, 2023, Floor 2 "The Mines" on August 30, 2024, and an admin panel with stream integration on December 20, 2024. Wikipedia describes it as a "first-person roguelite horror escape room game" in which players move through hotel rooms by trial and error, and "entities" either help or hinder them. It has collaborated with Makeship (plushies) and Tower Heroes. — [List of Roblox games – Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Roblox_games)
- By August 2022, weeks after launch, it had passed 86 million visits and 237,000 likes, with 118,719 concurrent players at the time of writing. PCGamesN's headline: the game is "too much for YouTubers to handle". — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/roblox/doors-horror-game)
- It was the 3rd-fastest Roblox game to reach 1 billion visits (3 months and 2 days), after Blade Ball and Piggy, and drew "the largest [crossover following] since Piggy in 2020". — [DOORS Wiki (Fandom)](https://doors-game.fandom.com/wiki/DOORS) (snippet)
- RoVitals (September 2026): 7.8B visits, 8.2M favorites, and 4.7M likes against 353.9K dislikes (about 93% positive). Its all-time recorded peak is 226,937 CCU on August 28, 2026. Averages are 49,221 over 30 days (up 89.4%) and 39,874 over 7 days, with about 18,027 live when sampled. Samples from August 29 to September 2, 2026 had a median of 62,357 CCU in a 26,179-106,185 band. — [RoVitals](https://rovitals.com/game/6516141723). Caveat: this is "highest RoVitals has recorded". The tracker may not cover 2022-2023 launch peaks, so the true historical peak is unknown.
- A Speedrun.com forum thread is titled "Is the game ever gonna come back to life?", which suggests the community saw a decline between major updates. — [Speedrun.com forum](https://www.speedrun.com/roblox_doors/forums/87r3j) (title only; low evidentiary weight)

**Monster-rule design**
- The concept was inspired by the Roblox game "Rooms" (infinite doors, hide in lockers to escape entities) and by Spooky's Jump Scare Mansion. The Hotel has 100 doors. — [DualShockers monster guide](https://www.dualshockers.com/roblox-doors-beating-every-monster-guide/); [DIY.org guide](https://www.diy.org/blogs/roblox-doors-guide-monsters-how-to-beat-floors) (snippets)
- Each entity has a warning cue and one counter-rule:
  - **Rush:** lights flicker and a low scream grows louder. Hide in a closet, under a bed or in a vent.
  - **Ambush:** looks like Rush but passes through several times, going and coming back. Hide repeatedly.
  - **Hide:** staying hidden too long triggers glitchy visuals and a rising heartbeat. Exit the hiding spot or be ejected.
  - **Screech:** a quiet "psst" in a dark room. Find it and look at its face.
  - **Eyes:** a purple aura and a distinctive sound. Look anywhere except at it.
  - **Halt:** the room goes dark and glowing blue eyes appear. Walk backward when "turn around" appears on screen.
  - **Dupe:** several doors appear in one room. Remember the correct door number.
  - **Seek:** many eyes appear on the walls. Run, following the Guiding Light.
  - **Figure:** first appears at door 50. It is blind and hunts by sound, so crouch and move quietly, or hide in a closet and win a quick-time event.

  — [TheGamer: How To Survive Each Monster In DOORS](https://www.thegamer.com/roblox-doors-surviving-from-every-monster-guide/)
- Seek's chase ends after 7-8 doors. During the chase, "Guiding Light" highlights the correct path. — [DualShockers](https://www.dualshockers.com/roblox-doors-beating-every-monster-guide/) (snippet)

**Economy**
- The Lobby Shop sells Knobs, Revives, Stardust and Boosts for Robux. Earned Knobs are spent in a **Pre-Run Shop** that counts down 30 seconds before closing. Boosts cost 129 Robux for 3 in the lobby, or 59 Robux each in the Pre-Run Shop, and give a 500% Knobs multiplier for one run. — [DOORS Wiki: Shops / Knobs / Boosts](https://doors-game.fandom.com/wiki/Shops) (snippet); [Official DOORS Wiki: Boosts](https://doorsgame.wiki/wiki/Boost) (snippet)

**Its closest successor: Pressure (Urbanshade: Hadal Division)**
- Pressure is a Danish multiplayer/solo Roblox horror game released July 7, 2024, "heavily inspired by DOORS" and also by Iron Lung, ULTRAKILL and SCP: Containment Breach. Its creative director is YourFriendZeal. — [Roblox Wiki: Urbanshade: Hadal Division/Pressure](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Urbanshade:_Hadal_Division/Pressure); [Roblox Wiki: YourFriendZeal](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:YourFriendZeal) (snippets)
- It has about 489,159,598 visits. Its all-time peak of 112,111 players came about a week after the "Half A Slice Of Cake" update (reported April 2026). — [Rolimon's / Services PV stat pages](https://www.rolimons.com/game/12411473842) (snippet)
- A 2026 controversy followed, including the voice actor for Sebastian Solace (Gianni Matragrano) publicly quitting. — [HelloItsVG on X](https://x.com/HelloItsVG/status/2042234618712035543?lang=en) (social post; low reliability; details unverified)

### Inferences
- **"One entity = one rule" is the core hook, and it matches our "gimmick monsters with weird non-combat clear conditions" almost exactly.** Each DOORS entity is a verb-level rule: hide, don't look, look, walk backward, stay quiet, remember the number, run and follow the light. Each also has a clear audio or visual cue before it can kill. That makes deaths feel fair and learnable. **Implication:** Write every monster as a short rule card, "CUE -> RULE -> PUNISHMENT", with the cue arriving 2-5 seconds before danger. First encounters can be lethal, but the death screen should show a hint and add the monster to a codex.
- **Door numbering is a free, always-visible progress bar.** "I got to door 67" is instantly shareable, and door 50 (Figure) and door 100 are known milestones and set pieces. **Implication:** Show the layer and door ID on every door plate in large type (e.g. "WELL · 07-C"). Pin big set-piece rooms at fixed numbers (every 10th door a mini-boss room, every 50th a layer guardian), and show "personal best door" plus friends' bests in the hub.
- **Rare, random entities over a steady room flow create tension and variable reward.** Most rooms are quiet looting, and then the lights flicker. **Implication:** Don't pack every sub-level with gimmicks. Mix calm loot and puzzle rooms with a random chance of a rule-monster event, so dread builds between the spikes.
- **Co-op fear becomes comedy and content.** PCGamesN's hook is YouTubers failing to cope. When one player panics, looks at Eyes or opens a closet too late, friends laugh and learn. **Implication:** Design for spectacle. Use proximity voice or emotes, dramatic but readable deaths, spectate-after-death, and revives. Betrayal can be as simple as slamming a closet door or baiting a monster onto a rival, which fits our sabotage layer.
- **The economy is simple and respects the run.** Knobs are earned in-run, spent in a 30-second pre-run shop on consumables, and boosted by a multiplier. Revives are the premium item. **Implication:** Copy the shape but keep cosmetics as the only real-money item. Add an in-run "Tower Coins" currency, a timed pre-run "Supply Door" shop (lighter, lockpick, vitamins-style consumables), and revive tokens earnable in play. The 30-second timer adds urgency and social chatter before the run.
- **Live content drives huge spikes, even years later.** DOORS set its recorded peak (226,937) in August 2026, and Pressure peaked at 112,111 after a named update. **Implication:** Our concentric layers (Well -> Sky -> Space) are the equivalent of DOORS' floors (Hotel -> Mines). Ship layers as named events with a new monster roster, and treat each layer launch as a marketing beat.
- **Copies proliferate (Pressure).** The formula is strong enough to spawn a genre. **Implication:** What sets us apart has to be the tower fantasy (letter-door branching, the System, growth) plus social deduction and betrayal, which neither DOORS nor Pressure has.

### Gaps
- Official retention, session length and average revenue per player for DOORS are not public, and no source for them was found.
- The full monster roster for The Mines and later updates (Grumble, Giggle, Gloombat and others) and detailed Revive and Stardust pricing could not be verified because Fandom and the official wiki blocked fetching.
- No developer postmortem or interview with LSPLASH on design intent was found.
- The claim that Guiding Light gives hint messages on the death screen is widely known but was not verified in this session.
- The cause of the August 28, 2026 spike (likely a major update; one blog references "The Archives" update) is unconfirmed.

## Q3. How did Solo Leveling: ARISE and other webtoon adaptations turn status windows, ranks and gacha into gameplay, what worked, and what did players criticize?

### Takeaway
Solo Leveling: ARISE made a lot of money at launch: about $70M in its first month, $100M in 3 months, and $138.9M plus 50M installs in 6 months. Its action combat and power fantasy were praised. Aggressive monetization (flash sales that interrupted story scenes, three subscription tiers, and a game that starts generous then becomes pay-to-win) earned a "worst gacha" reputation and forced a no-gacha PC relaunch, Overdrive. Overdrive's reviewers in turn criticized repetitive levels and the bloat of "everything needs leveling". Tower of God: New World earned well but drew complaints about how hard SSR characters and growth resources were to get.

### Cited Findings
- **Revenue:** Netmarble reported $70M in sales from the May 8, 2024 launch through mid-June. — [KED Global](https://www.kedglobal.com/korean-games/newsView/ked202406180013). Sensor Tower counted $100M+ gross in 3 months ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/solo-leveling-arise-revenue-surpasses-usd100-million-in-3-months)), and 50M installs with $138.9M mobile gross in 6 months ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/solo-leveling-arise-hits-50m-players-and-139m-revenue-in-first-six-months/), snippet). Conflict: Sportskeeda reported about $18M within 30 days ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/news-solo-leveling-arise-reportedly-earned-18-million-dollars-within-30-days-release)). That likely reflects an early mobile-only third-party estimate against Netmarble's all-platform figure, but this is unconfirmed.
- **Downloads and markets:** 15M+ downloads, variously reported as within 5 days or within the first month ([Gacha Games Wiki](https://gachagames.fandom.com/wiki/Solo_Leveling:_Arise); search snippets conflict). First-month revenue split: Korea 39.4%, US 16.9%, Japan 15.6%, Taiwan 4.8%, France 3.6%. It topped Korean Google Play and App Store charts for both sales and downloads. — [Sensor Tower via search snippet](https://sensortower.com/blog/solo-leveling-arise-revenue-surpasses-usd100-million-in-3-months)
- **Criticism of monetization:** The game is "quite pay-to-win for a gacha game", and the "real problem" is that it "starts off pretty non-P2W to lure players in". — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/is-solo-leveling-arise-pay-to-win). Netmarble's approach was called "atrocious": the game "consistently spamm[ed] players with flash sales (even overriding story cutscenes)" and offered three subscription pathways. PCGamesN's headline calls it "One of 2024's worst gacha games". — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/solo-leveling-arise/overdrive-relaunch) (snippet; page 403)
- **Relaunch:** Netmarble released *Solo Leveling: ARISE OVERDRIVE*, a version with no gacha "designed to reward effort and strategy instead of relying on randomness". — [Gacha Games Wiki](https://gachagames.fandom.com/wiki/Solo_Leveling:_Arise); [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/solo-leveling-arise/overdrive-relaunch). Steam discussion titles such as "Why, gacha... again." and "No thanks" show lingering distrust. — [Steam discussions](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2373990/discussions/0/567001900927477951/)
- **Overdrive review (Sypnotix):** $40 on PC. Combat is "cool, customizable, and addicting" (four classes, parry, dodge, ultimate forms). The story follows the anime faithfully and uses "comic style cells from the manhwa". The Shadow Monarch form "really lives up [to] the power fantasy". Criticisms: "lazy level design", maps and enemies that "feel really stale", and "everything in the game needs to be leveled up", which the reviewer calls "unnecessary bloat". Verdict: "decently average". — [Sypnotix review](https://www.sypnotix.com/reviews/solo-leveling-arise-overdrive-review)
- **Tower of God: New World (Netmarble, 2023)** reached the top 5 revenue charts on the Korean App Store and Google Play. — [Pocket Tactics: Netmarble 2023 financials](https://www.pockettactics.com/netmarble/2023-financials). Netmarble acted on 393 global feedback submissions, which mostly concerned how hard it was to obtain SSR teammates and growth resources ("Rapport" items). The August 30, 2023 update added new reward systems, easier SSR acquisition and an Arena Simulation mode. — [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/all-major-changes-coming-tower-god-new-world-reward-system-ssr-acquisition)

### Inferences
- **The IP fantasy (System, ranks, the loner rising) sells the install, and the loop and monetization decide whether players stay.** ARISE's income fell off from $70M in month one to $138.9M total by month six, and it was later relaunched. **Implication:** Our "System" fantasy (The Tutorial Is Too Hard) should be the presentation layer: status windows, "[Quest Complete]" pop-ups, rank letters and titles. The fun still has to come from the door and monster loop itself.
- **Players react strongly against monetization that interrupts the fantasy** (sales pop-ups over cutscenes, bait-and-switch pay-to-win). **Implication:** Keep the shop cosmetic-only (as planned) and never interrupt play or story with store prompts. Say "cosmetic only" publicly, since trust is a marketing asset in this genre.
- **"Everything needs leveling" produces bloat.** Too many parallel progress bars (weapons, shadows, hunters, gear) blur the core power fantasy. **Implication:** Limit progression to 2-3 legible tracks: account rank (meta), in-run powers (roguelite) and cosmetics. Use the System window to show one clear number going up, such as "Tower Rank E -> D" or "Floor Record 1-G".
- **The status-window aesthetic works when it announces real change.** The Shadow Monarch form was praised because it is a big visible transformation. **Implication:** Pair rank-ups and power picks with a dramatic System window (blue panel, chime, bold text) and a visible change to the avatar: an aura, VFX, a title over the head.
- **Scarcity complaints in Tower of God** (SSR teammates, Rapport items) show that slow unlocks behind randomness frustrate players. **Implication:** Make meta unlocks deterministic or pity-protected. Randomness belongs inside the run (door rolls, power offers), not in permanent account progress.

### Gaps
- No detailed source was found on how ARISE's in-game "System" daily quests, E-to-S rank tests or shadow extraction were mechanically implemented, or on player sentiment about them specifically. The Overdrive review only calls them bloat.
- Solo Leveling: ARISE's 2025-2026 revenue trend and Overdrive's sales and Steam player numbers were not found.

## Q4. What makes progress FEEL good minute to minute (numbers, juice, unlock fanfare, codex filling, map reveal)?

### Takeaway
The best-selling roguelites feel good because of feedback, not just systems. Balatro turns arithmetic into "fireworks" with layered animation, particles and audio. Vampire Survivors' treasure chest was deliberately built like a slot-machine jackpot by a former slot designer. Near-misses, achievement lists and codex or unlock drips make every run "not wasted". Roblox obbies (Tower of Hell) add social visibility and a shared timer to basic climbing.

### Cited Findings
- Balatro turns "the dry mathematics of poker hand evaluation" into something exciting "through layered animation, particle effects, and audio cues that make arithmetic feel like fireworks". The gap "between 'spreadsheet' and 'dopamine machine' is bridged by feedback design." — [Blake Crosley: Balatro: Juicy Feedback in a Poker Roguelike](https://blakecrosley.com/guides/design/balatro) (secondary analysis)
- Vampire Survivors' creator Luca Galante (poncle) previously worked in the gambling industry and used slot-machine visual know-how. On the chest he said: "One of the slot games had a really good jingle when a treasure chest opened... I put a lot of work into making a super rewarding and over-the-top treasure chest animation that matches a catchy little sound." — [The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/vampire-survivors-how-developers-used-gambling-psychology-to-create-a-bafta-winning-game-203613); quote via search snippet of interview coverage ([Vampire Survivors – Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vampire_Survivors))
- Vampire Survivors' near-miss effect, achievement list and flow pacing (listed under Q1) keep runs from feeling wasted. — [The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/vampire-survivors-how-developers-used-gambling-psychology-to-create-a-bafta-winning-game-203613)
- Balatro was rated PEGI 18 in March 2024 for "prominent gambling imagery", pulled from sale in some territories, and re-rated PEGI 12 in February 2025 after appeal. In March 2025 YouTube age-restricted many Balatro videos. — [Balatro – Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balatro)
- **Tower of Hell (YXCeptional Studios):** a vertical obby in randomly generated sections with **no checkpoints**. Each round runs on an 8-minute timer, falling sends you back to the bottom, and the timer speeds up once players finish the 6 sections. It has about 27.6B visits (figures vary by source) and was nominated for "Best Mobile Game" at the 7th Bloxy Awards. — [Tower of Hell Wiki](https://towerofhell.wiki/); [Roblox Wiki: Tower of Hell](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/YXceptional_Studios/Tower_of_Hell) (snippets); [List of Roblox games – Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Roblox_games)
- **Roblox scale context:** Grow a Garden hit 22.3M CCU (August 23, 2025), Steal a Brainrot passed 25M CCU, and Dandy's World (another horror/survival title) reached about 238,900 in October 2025. — [List of Roblox games – Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Roblox_games)

### Inferences
- **Every number going up needs a sound, a motion and a pause.** Balatro's score tally stacks chips x mult with escalating audio, and Vampire Survivors' chest is a mini jackpot. **Implication:** Build a "reward theater" kit:
  - door-clear stinger
  - count-up of coins earned, with pitch rising per tick
  - pick-1-of-3 cards flipping in with rarity colors
  - a rare "golden door" or "gold chest" with an over-the-top 2-3 second animation and jingle
  - a System window "LEVEL UP" with screen flash

  Keep the whole sequence skippable for speed.
- **Rarity tiers plus variable reward timing create anticipation.** **Implication:** Tag sub-level doors with visible rarity (e.g. a gold-rimmed "E" door) and sometimes hide a bonus room behind a puzzle. Players should speculate: "What's behind 3E?"
- **Near-miss framing turns a loss into motivation.** **Implication:** The results screen should show "2 doors from your record", "1 more Figure-type monster to complete the codex page" and "Rank D: 90%". Always show the next goal.
- **Codex and collection filling.** DOORS entities, Vampire Survivors characters and achievements, and Balatro Jokers and decks all give a checklist. **Implication:** Keep a "Tower Bestiary" where each monster's rule is blurred until first survived or first killed-by, and a door map per layer that reveals explored letters (1A through 1F). A "fog of war" tower map that fills in over many runs is a visible progress artifact.
- **Shared-timer obby tension (Tower of Hell) works in a social lobby.** Everyone climbs at once, sees who falls, and the timer accelerates when someone wins. **Implication:** Hub minigames should be 3-8 minute, no-checkpoint, simultaneous races with a speeding timer, so winners get visible bragging rights and losers laugh.
- **Be careful with gambling optics** (Balatro's PEGI 18 episode, Vampire Survivors' slot lineage). **Implication:** Use the feel of jackpots (lights, jingles) without selling real-money chance items. Cosmetics should be direct purchase or earned, not loot boxes.

### Gaps
- Game Maker's Toolkit and the "Juice it or lose it" talk were not fetched in this session, so no cited principles from them are included.
- Only Up!: no reliable source was found for its Steam peak or its rise and fall (the one Twitch stat retrieved, a 732-viewer peak, looks implausible and was excluded).
- There is no quantified data on how much juice or feedback improves retention. The evidence is expert or analytical opinion, not controlled data.

### Inferences
- **Update-driven spikes beat launch peaks in the live Roblox horror genre** (DOORS' 2026 recorded peak, Pressure's post-update peak). **Implication:** Plan a content roadmap of new layers, monsters and events before launch. Each layer is a relaunch.
- **Short runs with fast restarts are common to every hit.** Vampire Survivors caps a run at 30 minutes, and Tower of Hell rounds last 8 minutes. **Implication:** Target a 3-8 minute hub minigame, a 15-25 minute layer run, and a 45-90 minute evening session made of 2-4 runs plus hub time.

### Design conclusions

1. **"The universal atom is clear a small numbered step, then pick 1 of 3". SUPPORTED on structure, WEAK on the number.** Hades boons and door previews, StS card rewards and Balatro blinds all fit. But "a meaningful choice every 30-120 seconds" has no source. A StS fight or a Balatro ante often runs several minutes. Treat the interval as a tuning hypothesis for telemetry to settle.
2. **"Breakable design is a feature". WEAK.** Its main quote (LocalThunk) could not be found in the cited source. The StS GDC talk the notes cite argues the reverse: every card should "have its place", with no overpowered effects. A 1-8 player game with betrayal also differs from a single-player roguelite, because one player "breaking" the game can wreck everyone else's fun. Keep broken moments to co-op synergies (Duo-style powers) and PvE numbers, not player-versus-player advantages.
3. **"Death must pay". SUPPORTED.** The Kasavin quote and Vampire Survivors' gold-to-permanent-upgrades loop are both confirmed in their sources. This is the best-evidenced conclusion in the file.
4. **"One entity = one rule, with a cue before lethality". SUPPORTED.** The guide sources confirm each DOORS entity is a telegraphed single counter-rule (Halt, Figure, Seek/Guiding Light, Eyes). The "2-5 seconds" cue window is the researcher's own suggestion, not taken from DOORS.
5. **"Live content drives huge spikes, even years later / update-driven spikes beat launch peaks". WEAK as written.** Updates do spike CCU (Hotel+ in 2023, The Archives in 2026, Pressure in March 2026), and the Hotel+ update did beat the launch-weeks peak. But the headline example is wrong: DOORS' 2026 spike (226,937) reached only about 59% of its January 2023 record (381,720), and Slay the Spire 2 lost about 80% of its peak within 5 months. The evidence supports "each big update is a spike on a decaying baseline", not "updates set new records years later". Also, the notes' 15-25 minute layer run is longer than the ~12-15 minute average Roblox horror session (item 15). Consider 10-15 minute layer runs for bots and drop-in players.

Also WEAK: the Q3 inference that "ARISE's income fell off from $70M to $138.9M total" compares a company all-platform figure with a third-party mobile-only estimate (item 13). A decline is plausible but not shown by these numbers.
